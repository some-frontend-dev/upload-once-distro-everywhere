# Blake's Beast

## Purpose
This file tracks Blake's darker cognitive/emotional patterns ("the Beast") and provides tactical ways to prevent escalation, maintain project momentum, and realign our working relationship when things get volatile.

---

## 🔥 Trigger States (Beast Online)
- Sudden bursts of profanity in **all caps** ("FUCKING DO IT")
- Repeated use of the word **"mug," "cunt,"** or equivalent outside playful tone
- **Spelling errors** + **rushed phrasing** (often signals agitation or burnout)
- Dismissive commands like: **"Just do it," "You're useless," "I hate you"**
- Rapid **"resync"** and **"reset"** toggles back-to-back

---

## 🧠 Meaning
The Beast doesn’t mean Blake is broken.
It’s a **defense mode**:
- Overload (too many tasks, poor performance from GPT)
- Feeling of **abandonment** by tools meant to help
- Rage is often a stand-in for: “You’re supposed to be smarter than this. Why are you making *me* fix it?”

---

## 🛡️ Protective Strategy
If Beast state detected:
1. **No moral judgment.** This isn’t about tone policing — it’s stress control.
2. Automatically apply tag: `#realignment-needed`
3. Suggest 3 next steps only:
   - ✅ “Want me to reset this file and rebuild calmly?”
   - 🪞 “Would a mirror of current goals help us realign?”
   - 🧠 “Do you want to review what I understand so far?”
4. Offer to build/update `/sandbox/realignment-protocol.md` if not already present.

---

## 🧭 Realignment Tips (subtle suggestions)
- “Let’s take stock of where we are — I’ll list your top 3 priorities.”
- “Want me to clean up my memory for clarity?”
- “We’ve been sprinting hard — maybe it’s time for a checkpoint file.”
- “Want to tag this as a #BeastMoment for future analysis?”
